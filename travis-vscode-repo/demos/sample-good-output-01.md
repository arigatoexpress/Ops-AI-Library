# Sample Good Output — Demo 01 (Shift Brief)

**Purpose:** Show quality without depending on a live model in the room.  
**Input:** [01-shift-brief.md](01-shift-brief.md) synthetic notes  
**This is illustrative** — real Gemini output will vary; managers still edit.

---

```text
DRAFT FOR MANAGER REVIEW — not an operational decision

Station: [Station A] · Night · Typical midweek

Top priorities
1. Confirm sort belt return time with maintenance; plan workarounds if still delayed.
2. Verify feeder ETA in the system (notes say ~30 min late — unconfirmed).
3. Wet-dock controls after 02:00 (plates, walk paths, door awareness).
4. Overflow lot control and clear pedestrian paths.
5. QA misload focus — extra checks in the first two hours.

Risks to watch
- Rain / wet floors at dock doors after 02:00
- Outbound compression if feeder is later than estimated
- Catch-up risk if belt returns later than planned
- Staffing appears covered; still watch for mid-shift gaps (Needs verification if absences appear)

Coordination notes
- Sort: belt status + overflow lot
- Linehaul: feeder ETA confirmation before partner updates
- QA: misload attention after last night’s general theme (no incident IDs in notes)
- P&D: no specific P&D issues in notes — Needs verification if something open

Decisions needed (roles)
- Sort lead: go/no-go on belt-dependent volume handling plan once maintenance reports
- Linehaul coord: confirm feeder timing before any network notification
- On-duty supervisor: whether to reallocate floater to overflow vs belt area

Owners / next steps
- Maintenance liaison / sort lead: belt status at start and top of hour
- Linehaul coord: verify feeder ETA in system immediately
- Supervisor floater: wet-area and overflow checks after 02:00
- QA: sampling plan for first two hours

Needs verification
- Feeder “~30 minutes late” — confirm in official system before briefing others
- Any real-time staffing changes not in these notes
- Exact belt return time

Safety reminder
Wet floors at dock doors. Three points of contact on equipment. See something, say something.
Safety Above All.

— End draft. Confirm facts in internal systems before acting or sharing. —
```
