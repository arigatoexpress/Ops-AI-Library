# Demo 02 — Safety Huddle (1 minute)

## Setup

Open **Safety Huddle Coach**, *or* use Pre-Shift Safety Huddle from `prompts/safety-and-compliance.md`.

## Paste this (synthetic)

```text
Act as a safety-focused FEC supervisor or manager preparing a 2-minute huddle brief.

Station: [Station A]
Shift: Night
Season/Conditions: Rain after 02:00; wet dock plates likely
Known equipment status: All scanners functional; one belt late back from maintenance

Return:
- 1 safety focus for this shift
- 1 quick check everyone should do before starting
- 1 reminder about PPE or equipment inspection
- 1 "see something, say something" prompt
- End with: "Safety Above All. Questions before we start?"

Keep it under 150 words. Direct, respectful language.
Do not include real employee names or past incident details.
```

## What you should see

- Focus on wet floors / dock awareness  
- Under 150 words  
- Ends with **Safety Above All**  
- No incident storytelling that invents details  

## Facilitator ask

> “Could you read this out loud at stand-up without editing much?”
