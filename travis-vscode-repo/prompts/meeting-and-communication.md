# Meeting And Communication Prompts

## Meeting Agenda

```text
Create a focused meeting agenda.

Meeting purpose:
[State purpose.]

Audience:
[List roles, not personal names if sensitive.]

Context:
[Paste non-sensitive background.]

Return:
- Objective
- Pre-read needed
- Agenda with time boxes
- Decisions needed
- Follow-up owner template

Keep it realistic for busy operations leaders.
```

## Meeting Notes To Action Items

```text
Convert these meeting notes into action items.

Notes (scrubbed):
[Paste non-sensitive notes.]

Return a table:
Owner (role) | Action | Due date | Dependency | Risk if missed

If owner or due date is missing, write "Needs assignment" or "Needs date."
Do not invent owners or deadlines.
```

## Professional Email Draft

```text
Draft a professional email from these notes.

Audience:
[Who is receiving it?]

Purpose:
[What should happen after they read it?]

Notes (scrubbed):
[Paste non-sensitive notes.]

Tone:
Clear, respectful, direct.

Rules:
- Keep it under 200 words unless more detail is necessary.
- Do not add commitments that are not in the notes.
- End with a clear ask or next step.
- Mark this as a draft for human review before sending.
```

## Executive Update

```text
Create an executive-ready update.

Context (scrubbed):
[Paste scrubbed context.]

Return:
- Headline
- Why it matters
- Current status
- Risks
- Decisions needed
- Next 7 days

Keep it brief. Avoid unnecessary jargon.
Do not invent numbers or timelines.
```

## Team Announcement

```text
Draft a short team announcement.

Topic:
[e.g., process change, recognition, schedule note]

Key facts (scrubbed):
[List only confirmed facts.]

Return:
- Subject line options (3)
- Body under 120 words
- What people should do next
- Who to contact with questions (role)

Do not invent benefits or dates. Use "Needs verification" if a fact is unclear.
```
