# Customer And Contractor Prompts

**Extra care:** external-facing text can create commitments.  
Always human-review before sending. Never paste real customer or contractor personal data into unapproved tools.

## Service Alert Draft (Internal First)

```text
Draft an internal service alert that a manager can adapt.

Situation (scrubbed):
[General service impact — no tracking numbers or customer names]

Return:
- Internal summary (who needs to know)
- Customer-facing draft (optional section) that makes no unverified promises
- What must be verified before any external send
- Escalation path questions

Tone: calm, factual. Under 200 words per section.
```

## Escalation Response Outline

```text
Outline a response to a service escalation using scrubbed notes.

Notes:
[No customer PII, no tracking numbers]

Return:
- Empathy + ownership line (no admissions beyond known facts)
- What we know / what we are checking
- Next update timing (if known)
- Internal actions (roles)
- "Do not say" list (guesses, blame, unapproved compensation)

Human review required before any send.
```

## ISP / Contractor Briefing Draft

```text
Create a briefing outline for an ISP or contractor touchpoint.

Topics (scrubbed):
[e.g., volume outlook ranges, safety focus, process change]

Return:
- Agenda (15–20 minutes)
- Key messages
- Safety reminder
- Questions to expect
- Follow-ups with owners (roles)

No personal contractor data. No confidential network strategy.
```

## Team Recognition Note

```text
Draft a short recognition note for a team win.

Win (scrubbed):
[What went well — no sensitive metrics if restricted]

Return:
- 3 subject line options
- Body under 100 words
- Specific behaviors recognized (not vague praise)
- Safety or quality tie-in when relevant
```
