# Peak Season And Surge Prompts

Use general ranges and scenarios. Do not paste real staffing rosters, exact volumes, or contractor personal data.

## Pre-Peak Readiness Brief

```text
Create a pre-peak readiness brief for a station or hub manager.

Operation type: [Station / Sort Hub / P&D / mixed]
Peak window: [general dates]
Known constraints (scrubbed): [equipment, space, training, contractor capacity — general]
Lessons from last peak (scrubbed): [what slowed us down]

Return:
- Top 5 readiness priorities
- Top 5 risks with early warning signs
- Daily rhythm recommendations (plan / midday check / closeout)
- Cross-team coordination notes (sort, P&D, linehaul, quality)
- Open questions needing verification
- Safety focus for peak conditions

Under 400 words. Draft for human review.
```

## Surge Day Checklist

```text
Build a surge-day checklist for supervisors.

Surge type: [volume spike / weather / network disruption / other]
Shift: [Day/Evening/Night]

Return checklists for:
- Before shift
- First 60 minutes
- Mid-shift recovery
- End-of-shift handoff

Each item: checkbox + why it matters (one short phrase).
Include a safety line in every section.
No real names, rosters, or package identifiers.
```

## Staffing Scenario Discussion (Non-Sensitive)

```text
Help structure a staffing scenario discussion using ranges only.

Scenario: [e.g., "volume ~15% above typical Tuesday"]
Available capacity notes (ranges/roles only): [e.g., "overtime available for package handlers; contractor vans constrained"]
Constraints: [training, equipment, space]

Return:
- Options table: Option | Pros | Cons | Risks | Info still needed
- What must be verified in official systems before any decision
- What NOT to decide from this chat alone

Do not invent headcount numbers. Do not recommend violating labor policy.
```

## Post-Peak After-Action

```text
Create a post-peak after-action outline from scrubbed notes.

Notes:
[Paste non-sensitive notes.]

Return:
- What went well
- What broke or slowed flow
- Root causes to investigate
- Fixes before next peak (quick wins vs longer projects)
- Metrics to keep watching
- Recognition notes (roles/teams, not sensitive individual performance data)

Separate facts from opinions.
```
