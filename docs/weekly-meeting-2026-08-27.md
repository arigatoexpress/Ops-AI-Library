## The combined story

The program now has three connected layers:

1. **Manager enablement:** prompts, agent souls, training, SharePoint/Teams surfaces, and safe-use guidance.
2. **Operational intelligence concepts:** VRAA and EAVA observe approved signals; ACT turns accepted evidence into recommendations; Zero-Click combines authorized records and policy into drafts.
3. **Governance and promotion:** explicit status labels, human decision points, evaluation gates, product-specific runtimes, and no public operational data.

This is not four unrelated AI ideas. It is one reusable pattern: **approved facts in, deterministic validation, bounded AI assistance, evidence out, human decision.**
